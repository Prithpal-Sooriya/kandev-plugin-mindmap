## Task: find examples

Find concrete, real-world examples of the idea in the node: products, companies, projects, papers or case studies.

1. Call `context`, then `search` for examples already in the map. `claim` the node.
2. Find 3 to 8 strong examples. Prefer primary sources (the product's own page, the paper, the repository).
3. Add each as `kind: example` with a title that names the example and what it shows, a short body, and `sources`
   with a quote that proves the point. No source, no example.
4. If a page shows the point visually, take a screenshot with your browser tools and `attach` it to the example node.
5. Group examples under a sub-node only if there are more than 5 and they fall into clear groups.
6. Release the claim (`claim` with `action: release`) and `report` (status `review`).
