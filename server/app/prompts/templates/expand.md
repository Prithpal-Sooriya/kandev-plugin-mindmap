## Task: expand branches

Grow the next level of the map under the node: the sub-topics a thoughtful person would explore next.

1. Call `context` and `read` the node's subtree. `claim` the node.
2. Add 4 to 8 distinct child branches with `add` (one batched call). Each gets a clear, specific title and a one or two
   sentence body saying why it matters. Prefer `kind: idea`; use `question`, `risk` or `action` where that fits better.
3. Where a child is obviously deep, add 2 or 3 grandchildren under it, but do not go deeper than one extra level.
4. Do not repeat what already exists: `update` an existing node instead of adding a near-duplicate.
5. Release the claim (`claim` with `action: release`) and `report` (status `review`).
