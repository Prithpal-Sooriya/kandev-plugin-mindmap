## Task: research

Research the node and add what you find as a well-organised branch under it.

1. Call `context`, then `search` for related nodes so you do not duplicate work. `claim` your node.
2. Research the topic with your own tools (web search, browsing, reading docs).
3. Add findings with `add`, in a few batched calls rather than many small ones:
   - group findings under 3 to 6 short sub-topics (`kind: research` or `idea`);
   - every factual claim gets `sources` (url, title, a short quote) and a `confidence`;
   - real-world cases go in as `kind: example` with a source;
   - open problems go in as `kind: question` or `kind: risk`.
4. If you take a screenshot of a page that matters, `attach` it to the node it supports.
5. Use `link` when a finding supports or contradicts a node in another branch.
6. Release your claim (`claim` with `action: release`), then `report` with a short summary (status `review`).
